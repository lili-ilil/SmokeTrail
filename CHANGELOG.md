# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (12 November 2026)

First version.

- Scroll story in 12 stops over one panoramic landscape with a moving camera: ignition, inhalation, exhalation, wind, rain, clothes and hair, thirdhand smoke, butts, soil and plants, water, bystanders, Switzerland.
- Close-up lenses for the burning coal, the lungs, particle sizes (to scale), raindrop scavenging, thirdhand smoke chemistry and the butt.
- Charts for coal temperatures, retention in the lungs, sidestream/mainstream ratios, PM2.5 by distance, rain scavenging by particle size, skin uptake, plant growth, concentrations in water and health risks.
- Calculator for one cigarette, a pack a day, 20 years or Switzerland (7.96 billion cigarettes taxed in 2024), with ranges for sidestream emissions and for what the butts leave behind.
- Methods and limits section, 85 numbered sources, light and dark themes, reduced-motion support, phone layout.
